# Downcard marketing copy

App name: Downcard
Core truth: Simple, fun poker.
Voice: warm, simple, sincere. Poker night should be fun, not a casino that eats your money.
Avoid: cocky punchlines, clever antithesis ("X is fake, Y is real"), snark, and any claim we can't stand behind.

## Positioning

Downcard is the honest answer to every rigged-feeling, casino-style poker app. Three pillars, all true:

1. **No house.** The app never plays against you, never takes a rake, and you can't cash out. There's
   nothing for us to gain by you losing.
2. **Open source.** The whole game is public code, so you can check the deal is fair and the bots don't
   cheat. No casino app can say that.
3. **Simple and fun.** Clean, quick, play-with-friends poker. It's poker night, not a casino floor.

Honesty note: "open source" is a promise. It needs a real license + a visible repo link before we say it
publicly, and we claim "open, go read it," not "mathematically proven fair" (public code doesn't prove the
store build matches). Same goes for "bots can't see your cards / nothing's rigged," which is only true
as long as the shipped code keeps it true.

## App Store description

Simple, fun poker.

Downcard is No-Limit Texas Hold'em you can play with your friends in a couple of taps. Start a table, share the code, and you're dealing. That's the whole thing.

There's no real money in Downcard, and there's no house. The app never plays against you, never takes a rake, and you can't cash out, so nobody leaves the table in a worse spot than they showed up in. It's poker night, not a casino floor.

We're also open source. The whole game is public code, so you can check for yourself that the deal is fair and the bots aren't cheating. No casino app can offer that.

Nobody free to play? Deal in the bots. There are five of them, each plays a little differently, they're always labeled as bots, and they can't see your cards. When your friends aren't online you might wait for a public table to fill, but the bots are ready whenever you are, so you never have to wait to play.

Play a hand or play all night. Earn XP, climb the ranks, and track every hand as you go. Leave the table whenever you want and keep your chips for next time.

Simple, fun poker. Come play.

Features:
- Texas Hold'em with friends in a couple of taps
- Share a 6-character code to start a private table
- Five honest bots that play fair and can't see your cards
- No house, no rake, no cash-out, no ads
- Open source, so you can check the game is fair
- XP, ranks, and stats to track your game
- Free on iPhone and Android

## Open-source badge (a sticker, not a frame)

Instead of spending a whole screenshot on open source, put it on a small badge. It reads as a trust
marker at a glance without asking a casual player to care about reading code. The deeper "verify the
deal yourself" story still lives in the App Store description and in community and Reddit posts, where
the people who actually care about it are.

Placement: the hero (frame 1) gets it the most eyes and makes it a brand-level trust signal from the
first impression. The bots screen (frame 4) is the runner-up, since that is where "are the bots
cheating?" lives.

Badge copy options (short, with a hint of why it matters, not just membership):
- Open source
- 100% open source
- Open source. Check the code.

Skip "proud member of the open-source community" on its own. It signals belonging but not the reason it
matters, which is that anyone can check the game is fair.
