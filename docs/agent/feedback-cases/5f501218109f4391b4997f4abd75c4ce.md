## Working theory
On an MP hand that reaches showdown (multiple players to the river, not a
fold-to-one), the client advances River→Complete→next hand without surfacing the
showdown (opponent hole cards + winning hand). Either the server isn't sending a
showdown-reveal frame, or the client's `RemotePokerSession`/`PlayPokerViewModel`
doesn't hold on Complete long enough to render it before the next `game_state` for
the following hand lands (note hand 5 Preflop arrives only ~4s after Complete).
Fix likely needs a showdown-reveal step gated on "hand ended at showdown" that the
table holds before dealing the next hand. Confidence medium — needs the server-side
room log to decide server-emit vs client-render.
