## Working theory
Purely a client render-state transition bug in the hole-card / player-hand composable. After the reporter folds, the *next* hand deals but the folded player's hole-card view is briefly left in a raised/"ghost" placement (the up-top position) and only settles to its resting spot once it becomes the reporter's turn to act. Reads like the hand-area position/animation keys off `isActing` / turn state rather than off deal-completion, so between "new hand dealt" and "my turn" the card container renders in the wrong slot with a ghost styling. Non-blocking (self-corrects on the reporter's turn), hence P2. Fix lives in the player-hand / hole-card placement in `features/room/impl/.../ui/` (PlayerArea / hole-card composable); reproduce with a solo hand where the reporter folds, then assert the hole-card container's placement/visibility across the deal→pre-turn window in the next hand.
